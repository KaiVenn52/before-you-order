B Before You Order

Privacy Policy

Effective 31 August 2026

Overview

Before You Order helps people choose a meal before placing an order. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information we collect

Before You Order does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

Information stored on your device

Your meal choices, feedback, recent history, interface language, and hidden-food list are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to personalize suggestions and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

You can remove locally stored information by uninstalling the app. Hidden meals can also be restored within the app.

Google Maps and external links

When you choose to find a restaurant, Before You Order opens a text search in Google Maps or your browser. The app does not request device location permission or receive your location. Activity after opening Google Maps or another website is governed by that service's privacy policy.

The app also links to image sources and licenses for attribution. Those links open third-party websites.

Children's privacy

Before You Order is a general-audience meal decision tool and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before the relevant app update is released.
