# Privacy Policy for Before You Order

Effective date: 27 August 2026

Before You Order is designed to help users choose a meal before placing an order.

## Information we collect

Before You Order does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Information stored on your device

Your meal choices, preference settings, feedback, and blacklist are stored locally on your device so the app can improve future suggestions. This information is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

You can remove the app's locally stored information by uninstalling the app. Blacklisted meals can also be restored from within the app.

## Google Maps and external links

When you choose to find a restaurant, Before You Order opens a search query in Google Maps or your browser. The app does not request device location permission or receive your location. Your interaction with Google Maps or any linked website is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

The app also provides links to image sources and licenses for attribution. Opening those links takes you to the relevant third-party website.

## Children's privacy

Before You Order is a general-audience meal decision tool and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before the relevant app update is released.
